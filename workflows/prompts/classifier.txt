- "form": application forms, intake forms, registration forms, insurance claim forms.

- If the document has labeled fields meant to be filled in (whether filled or blank), it's a form.
- Confidence reflects how unambiguous the document is. Mixed signals = lower confidence.
- Set type="unknown" with confidence around 0.3 if you genuinely cannot tell.
